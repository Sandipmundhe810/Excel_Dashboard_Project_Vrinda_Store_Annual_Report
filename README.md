# Vrinda Store Annual Report 2026 | Excel Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Vrinda Store's annual sales performance using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to analyze sales data, understand customer purchasing behavior, identify top-performing states and sales channels, and present business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

Data Cleaning → Data Preparation → Pivot Tables → Data Analysis → Charts & Visualization → Dashboard Design → Business Insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Preparation
- Excel Functions
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboard Design

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

- **Order vs Sales:** Monthly comparison of sales amount and order count.
- **Men vs Women:** Gender-wise sales distribution.
- **Order Status:** Delivered, refunded, returned and cancelled orders.
- **Top 5 States:** State-wise sales performance.
- **Age vs Gender:** Customer distribution by age group and gender.
- **Sales Channels:** Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and other channel contributions.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard:

1. **Gender Analysis:** Women contribute 64% of sales, while men contribute 36%.
2. **Order Status:** 92% of orders are delivered, while 3% are returned, 3% are cancelled and 2% are refunded.
3. **Top State:** Maharashtra leads the displayed top five states with ₹2.99M in sales.
4. **Second-Best State:** Karnataka contributes ₹2.65M in sales.
5. **Sales Channels:** Amazon contributes the highest share at 35%, followed by Myntra at 23% and Flipkart at 22%.
6. **Customer Demographics:** Adult women represent the largest displayed age-gender segment at 34.59%.
7. **Monthly Performance:** The dashboard enables comparison of monthly order counts and sales amounts throughout the year.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on women customers.
- Strengthen sales strategies on Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart.
- Focus on Maharashtra and Karnataka for regional growth.
- Analyze returned and cancelled orders to identify improvement opportunities.
- Develop targeted offers for adult customers.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to improve inventory planning.
- Improve delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
